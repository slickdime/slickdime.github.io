Frequently asked questions

I didn't get my sign-in code.

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure the email address is spelled correctly. Codes expire after 10 minutes; tap "Use a different email" and request a new one. Wait about a minute between requests.

Some details on a scanned card are wrong.

Open the card and tap Edit to fix them. For better results, scan in good light, keep the card flat, avoid glare from overhead lights or the flash, and retake blurry photos when BizCard asks.

How do I turn AI on or off?

Go to Settings → AI card reading. With AI off, cards are read on your phone only and the text is never sent to the AI service.

The card photo is sideways.

Open the card and tap Rotate under the photo until it's upright.

I bought Remove ads but still see ads.

Go to Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same BizCard account and the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I move my contacts to another app?

Settings → Export contacts (vCard) for Apple Contacts, Google Contacts or Outlook, or Export spreadsheet (CSV) for Excel and CRMs. You can also save a single card with "Save to phone Contacts".

Do my cards sync between phones?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on each phone. Cards you add offline sync when you're back online.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. See deleting your account for details and other options.
