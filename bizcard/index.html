BizCard

Your business cards, in one searchable wallet. Scan a card, let AI fill in the details, and reach anyone in one tap.

  • Scan in secondsUse the camera or a photo. Name, title, company, email, website and address are filled in, and phone numbers are labelled Cell or Office.
  • Your real cardsSee the card photos in your wallet, framed by category colour. Flip front and back, rotate and zoom.
  • Reach people fastCall, text or email in one tap. People you contacted most recently rise to the top of each category.
  • Find anyoneSearch by name, company, city or category, or ask in plain words, like "realtors I met in Phoenix".
  • Notes and remindersRemember where you met and get a nudge to follow up.
  • Yours to keepWorks offline, syncs across your phones, and exports to CSV, vCard or your phone's Contacts.

Free, with an option to remove ads

BizCard is free with ads. A one-time in-app purchase removes them on all your devices.

Privacy at a glance
