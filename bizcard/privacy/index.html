BizCard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the BizCard mobile app ("BizCard", "the app") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. BizCard is provided by SLICK DIME LLC ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at apps.bizcard@gmail.com.

We do not read or upload your phone's address book. BizCard only writes a contact to your phone's Contacts when you tap "Save to phone Contacts". The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or import a card.

Information about other people

Business cards contain other people's contact details. We store and process them only to provide BizCard to you, and we never sell them or use them to contact those people. Please only add cards you received in the normal course of business.

AI features

The text on a card is first read on your phone. If you agree, BizCard sends that text (not the photo) to OpenRouter, which runs Anthropic's Claude AI model, to fill in the card's fields and suggest a category. When you ask a question in Search, the question and your list of category, tag and event names are sent the same way. BizCard asks for your permission before the first time, and you can turn AI off at any time in Settings → AI card reading. With AI off, cards are read on your phone only.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run BizCard for us, each limited to what it needs:

  • Supabase: stores your account, cards, photos and notes, and sends sign-in emails.
  • OpenRouter and Anthropic: process card text and search questions when AI is on.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads. BizCard requests non-personalized ads; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent as required by law.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google: process and confirm the Remove ads purchase. We never see your payment card details.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers sign-in code emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Advertising and tracking

BizCard shows ads from Google AdMob and asks only for non-personalized ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the app does not show a tracking permission prompt. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

How we protect and keep information

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own data, enforced by the database itself. We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your cards, photos, notes and profile are deleted from our servers right away; copies in our service providers' backups are removed within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: export your contacts any time from Settings (CSV or vCard).
  • Correct: edit any card in the app.
  • Delete: delete single cards in the app, or your whole account in Settings → Delete account. See how to delete your account.
  • AI: turn AI card reading on or off in Settings.
  • Ads: remove ads with a one-time purchase; EEA, UK and Swiss users can manage ad consent in Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EEA or the UK), you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Email us at apps.bizcard@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days. You will not be treated differently for using these rights.

Children

BizCard is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
