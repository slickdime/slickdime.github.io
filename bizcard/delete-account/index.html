Delete your BizCard account

BizCard by SLICK DIME LLC

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BizCard and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email apps.bizcard@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to BizCard, with the subject "Delete my BizCard account". We confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • All your cards, card photos, notes, reminders, categories, tags and events
  • Your purchase status in BizCard. The purchase record itself stays with Apple or Google, and you can restore it if you create a new account with the same store account.

Data is removed from our servers immediately. Copies in our service providers' backups are removed within 30 days. Contacts you exported or saved to your phone's Contacts are not affected.
